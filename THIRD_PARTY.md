# Third-party integration

StylePilot interoperates with
[`Automaat/lightroom-mcp`](https://github.com/Automaat/lightroom-mcp), copyright
its contributors and distributed under the MIT License.

- Included fork: <https://github.com/kotvaer/lightroom-mcp>
- Upstream base tag: `v0.9.0`
- Upstream base commit: `023c60abab69ebe29f198fd5e832ec1d20be125d`
- StylePilot fork commit: `593c0a4`
- Upstream license: <https://github.com/Automaat/lightroom-mcp/blob/v0.9.0/LICENSE>

The fork is included as the `vendor/lightroom-mcp` git submodule. Its original
MIT license and copyright notice remain in the submodule, while the fork history
identifies the StylePilot changes.
